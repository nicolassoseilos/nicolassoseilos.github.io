Privacy policy

Last updated 19 September 2026

Agentium ("we", "us") builds AI phone assistants for businesses in Cyprus. This policy explains what information our voice agents handle, why, and what your rights are. It covers callers who speak to an Agentium agent and the businesses that use one.

1. Who is responsible

Agentium is operated by Nicolas Soseilos, Nicosia, Cyprus. For anything in this policy, write to soseilos.nicholas@gmail.com. The business you are calling remains responsible for its own customer records; we process information on its behalf to run the phone assistant.

2. What the phone assistant collects when you call

  • Your phone number, as delivered by the telephone network.
  • What you tell the assistant — typically your name, the appointment you want, and any message you leave.
  • The conversation itself. The call is transcribed so the assistant can understand you. Calls may also be recorded; when they are, the assistant says so at the start of the call and you can ask it to stop recording at any time.

We do not collect payment details, identity documents or health records over the phone beyond what is needed to book the appointment you ask for.

3. What we do with it

  • Book, change or cancel your appointment in the business's calendar.
  • Keep a simple visit record for the business so that returning callers are recognised.
  • Pass a message to the business when the assistant cannot help.
  • Check the quality of the assistant's answers and fix problems.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

4. Google user data

To run the phone assistant for a business, we connect to that business's Google account with its permission. We ask for two things only:

  • Google Calendar — to read the business's availability and to create, change and cancel appointments that callers ask for.
  • Send email with Gmail — to send operational alert emails from the business's own account to the business (for example, that the assistant restarted or that a booking needs attention). We do not read, search or delete email.

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the phone assistant to the business that granted access. It is not transferred to anyone else except as needed to provide that service, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or acquisition; it is not used for advertising; and it is not read by a person except with the business's consent, for security reasons, to comply with the law, or in a form that does not identify anyone. The business can withdraw this access at any time from its Google account's third-party access settings.

5. Who else handles the information

The assistant runs on servers we rent and on services we use to make it work. Each of them processes information only on our instructions:

  • Vultr — the servers the assistant runs on.
  • Zadarma — the telephone line the calls arrive on.
  • Google — the speech and language model that understands and answers the caller, and the Calendar and Gmail services described above.
  • Airtable — where the business's customer and call records are kept.

Some of these providers process data outside the European Union; they do so under standard contractual clauses or an equivalent legal basis.

6. How long we keep it

  • Call recordings: at most 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Call transcripts: at most 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Appointment and visit records: for as long as the business remains a customer and needs them, after which they are deleted or returned to the business.

7. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to how it is used, or ask for a copy of it. Email soseilos.nicholas@gmail.com and we will answer within one month. You can also complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date at the top.
