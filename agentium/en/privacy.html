Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Who we are

Agentium makes voice agents, digital receptionists that answer the phone for businesses in Cyprus. This policy explains what personal data this website collects, why, and what rights you have.

Controller: Agentium. Privacy contact: soseilos.nicholas@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you book a demo: your full name, business name and type, phone, email and the time you picked.

When you try our voice agent: your voice is streamed live so it can answer you. We keep neither your voice nor the conversation text after the call ends, only an anonymous per-visitor time counter for the daily limit.

In your browser: your language, currency and cookie choices. They stay on your device.

Why we use it

To book and hold the demo you asked for and send you the link (legal basis: steps you asked for before a contract).

To run the voice agent demo and protect it from abuse (legitimate interest).

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only the providers this site needs to work: website hosting, calendar and email (for the demo invitation), and the voice services behind the demo. Some of them may process data outside the EU, under the EU standard contractual clauses. Ask us for the list.

How long we keep it

We keep a demo booking's details for 12 months, then delete them, unless you become a client.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete, restrict or move your data, and object to its use. Write to us at soseilos.nicholas@gmail.com.

You can also complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection (Cyprus).

Cookies

We only use what is necessary: your language, currency and cookie choices. We use no visitor counting or advertising cookies today. If that changes, we will ask you first. You can change your choices any time from “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page.

Changes

If this policy changes, so will the date at the top.
