AI voice agents that answer the phone for Cyprus businesses

Agentium builds phone assistants that pick up every call, book and change appointments, and take messages — in Greek and English — so a business never misses a customer because nobody was free to answer.

What an Agentium agent does

  • Answers incoming calls 24 hours a day, including when the line is busy or the business is closed.
  • Books, moves and cancels appointments directly in the business's calendar.
  • Recognises returning customers and keeps a simple record of visits.
  • Takes messages and passes on anything it cannot handle itself.

Who we work with

Small businesses that live by their phone line: dental and medical clinics, salons and spas, workshops and local services. Our first deployment is a dental clinic in Nicosia.
